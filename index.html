About & Archive

Md. Mahfuz Al Maruf

Based in Rajshahi, Bangladesh and an alumnus of Rajshahi Collegiate School, I document wildlife, historic architecture, lunar phases, and everyday human moments under Fleeting Time Machine.

My visual approach favors natural color grading, unmanipulated astronomical scale, and quiet atmospheric restraint—letting subjects speak with clarity before layering in future academic, design, and software work.
